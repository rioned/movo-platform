Core services

Two services. One standard of care.

Parcel delivery

Same-day movement of small and medium items across Kigali — for individuals, retailers and SMEs who can't justify a dedicated fleet.

  • B2C retail and pharmacy fulfilment
  • C2C gifts, personal items and forgotten belongings
  • SME orders from Instagram, WhatsApp and marketplaces

Document delivery

Secure, trackable same-day movement of documents that still require physical signatures, seals or submission.

  • Banks, insurers and law firms
  • Government, tenders and procurement
  • Universities, healthcare and corporate offices
