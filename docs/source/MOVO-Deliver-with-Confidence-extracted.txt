Executive Summary 
MOVO is an asset-light digital logistics marketplace designed to modernize same-day parcel and 
document delivery in Kigali. The platform connects customers and businesses with verified 
independent motorcycle riders, replacing informal coordination through phone calls and 
messaging with transparent pricing, digital booking, live tracking, proof of delivery, and structured 
rider management. 
Rwanda provides a strong launch environment for MOVO because the country combines rapid 
urbanization, deepening mobile adoption, supportive digital transformation policy, expanding 
digital financial services, and a large base of motorcycle riders already e mbedded in daily urban 
mobility. Kigali’s population reached 1,745,555 in the 2022 census, while Rwanda’s national 
population reached 13,246,394, creating a growing urban demand base for reliable last -mile 
logistics. 
MOVO’s marketplace model is deliberately different from a traditional courier fleet. It does not 
require heavy investment in owned vehicles or large delivery payrolls. Instead, MOVO supplies 
the digital infrastructure, trust layer, operating standards, cus tomer support, pricing logic, and 
transaction management that allow independent riders to serve individuals and organizations 
professionally. 
Positioning statement: MOVO is Rwanda’s Trusted Digital Logistics Platform—built to help 
customers, businesses, and verified independent motorcycle riders Deliver with Confidence 
through fast, secure, transparent, and professionally managed same-day logistics.

Why MOVO Matters

convenience, businesses need speed, and institutions still require secure movement of physical 
documents. In Kigali, the demand exists, but delivery coordination ofte n remains fragmented

MOVO matters because it converts fragmented delivery demand into a structured marketplace.

income opportunities, and public stakeholders a more formalized, data -enabled logistics 
ecosystem.

The Logistics Problem in Kigali 
Kigali’s logistics market is active but still operationally fragmented. Many deliveries are arranged 
through informal rider contacts, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or ad hoc referrals. While this 
approach can work for occasional deliveries, it creates several structural gaps on a scale. 
• Pricing inconsistency: customers often negotiate delivery fees without standardized zones 
or transparent estimates. 
• Limited visibility: senders and recipients may not know where the rider is, when delivery 
will arrive, or whether the item has been handed over. 
• Trust gaps: informal arrangements make it difficult to verify rider identity, track disputes, 
or establish repeatable service standards. 
• Manual coordination burden:  businesses spend staff time calling riders, confirming 
pickups, following up with customers, and recording delivery outcomes. 
• Missed rider productivity: riders may spend time waiting for passenger trips while nearby 
delivery demand remains unorganized. 
The MOVO Solution 
MOVO provides a standardized digital layer for same-day logistics. Customers request deliveries 
through an app; the platform calculates pricing, matches the request with a verified independent 
rider, tracks the journey, confirms delivery, processes payment , and records the transaction. 
Businesses can use MOVO as a professional logistics partner without hiring riders or owning 
motorcycles.

How MOVO Works 
1. Request Customer enters pickup, destination, item type, recipient details, 
and preferred payment method. 
2. Price MOVO calculates a transparent delivery fee using zones, distance, 
service type, urgency, and operating rules. 
3. Match Nearby verified riders receive the request; the platform assigns or 
confirms the most suitable rider. 
4. Pickup The rider verifies the pickup using code, photo, or app confirmation 
before collecting the parcel or document. 
5. Track The sender and recipient receive live updates, status notifications, and 
estimated arrival information. 
6. Deliver The recipient confirms delivery through OTP, digital signature, photo 
confirmation, or approved proof of delivery. 
7. Settle Payment is completed, MOVO applies the platform fee, rider earnings 
are recorded, and the delivery is stored in the customer history.

1. Introduction 
MOVO is a digital logistics platform designed to connect individuals and businesses with verified 
independent motorcycle riders for fast, secure, and transparent parcel and document delivery. 
The MOVO platform will manage the complete delivery process, from booking and pricing to 
rider assignment, live tracking, payment, proof of delivery, and customer support. 
MOVO will initially launch in Kigali with two principal services: 
• Parcel Delivery 
• Document Delivery 
The platform will operate as an asset-light digital marketplace. MOVO will not need to own a large 
delivery fleet. Instead, verified riders will use their own motorcycles and access delivery 
opportunities through the MOVO Rider Application. 
The technology platform will consist of: 
• MOVO Customer Application 
• MOVO Rider Application 
• MOVO Business Portal 
• MOVO Operations and Administration Portal 
• Central Backend Platform 
• Payment and Communication Integrations 
• Mapping, GPS, and Location Services 
• Reporting and Analytics Systems 
The platform must be secure, scalable, easy to use, and adapted to the operational realities of 
Kigali.

10. Core Delivery Lifecycle 
Every MOVO delivery should pass through a standardized lifecycle. 
Stage 1: Request Creation 
The customer provides the pickup, destination, service type, and delivery details. 
Stage 2: Pricing 
The system calculates the customer price and rider earnings. 
Stage 3: Rider Matching 
The platform identifies eligible riders and sends the request. 
Stage 4: Rider Assignment 
A rider accepts and is assigned to the delivery. 
Stage 5: Pickup 
The rider travels to the pickup location and verifies collection. 
Stage 6: In Transit 
The rider transports the parcel or document while the system records location updates. 
Stage 7: Delivery 
The rider arrives at the destination and verifies the recipient. 
Stage 8: Completion 
The system confirms delivery and generates proof of delivery. 
Stage 9: Settlement 
Payment is allocated between the rider and MOVO according to the configured pricing model. 
Stage 10: Rating and Support 
The customer rates the delivery and may report any issue.
